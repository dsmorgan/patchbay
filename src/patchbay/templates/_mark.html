{# The patchbay mark: two five-port panels, cross-patched. The teal cord runs the full width, from the top panel's port 1 to the bottom panel's port 5; the purple one crosses it on a run half as long, from the top's port 4 to the bottom's port 2. Each cord carries its own color, and its plugs match, so you can follow either one end to end. The port pairs are deliberately NOT mirror images (1->5 mirrors to 5->1, which is not what the second cord does), so the mark doesn't fold in half down the middle. The differing cord lengths are the point; four plugs on non-adjacent ports keep them spread across both panels. The second cord is drawn twice: once in the page background color and wider (.mk-case), then in its own color. That opens a gap where it passes over the first cord — without it the two cords merge into one knot at the crossing. Colors come from the theme via CSS (see _style.html). Two other copies carry literal colors because they inherit no CSS: the favicon in base.html, and docs/mark.svg, which the README shows. Change the drawing here and change all three. Keep every `/>` intact. The HTML parser only treats a tag as self-closing in SVG when the slash and the bracket are adjacent, so breaking the line between them leaves the element open and swallows the rest of the mark. #}
