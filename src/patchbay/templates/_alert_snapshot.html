{# Snapshot on critical (#66, ADR-0003 Decision 7): the two parameters, on the Rules tab. A plain form posting to /alerts/snapshot. #}
snapshot on critical

A critical alert that is raised, or escalated to critical, and sent to a transport writes a -alert snapshot after the poll, at most once per cooldown whatever raised it. Alert snapshots are kept under their own count, never the tiers, and are listed on Snapshots.
